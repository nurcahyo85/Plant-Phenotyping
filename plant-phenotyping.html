1. Executive Summary

Morphometric analysis was performed on 12 microscope images from 3 different specimens. A total of 847 cells were successfully detected and measured with 94% segmentation accuracy. 3. Conclusion

Cell morphology shows shade leaf characteristics with large epidermal cells, moderate stomatal density, and spongy mesophyll dominance. The stomata index of 0.14 is consistent with values reported for tropical shade-adapted species, indicating efficient gas exchange in low-light conditions.
